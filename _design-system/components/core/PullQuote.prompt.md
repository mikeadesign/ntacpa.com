Use for the tagline, a client quote, or a section-opening statement.

```jsx
<PullQuote>Where regulatory expertise meets personal service.</PullQuote>
<PullQuote tone="inverse" attribution="Nicholas T. Avello, CPA">
  Always, Never, Except For…
</PullQuote>
```

Spectral italic behind a gold rule — the only place italic display type appears
in the system. Keep quotes to one or two lines; this treatment loses its force
at paragraph length.
