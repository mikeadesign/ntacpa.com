Use to group content — a service, a credential, a contact block.

```jsx
<Card>Individual tax preparation</Card>
<Card tone="sunken" accent>Hedge fund &amp; brokerage accounting</Card>
<Card tone="inverse">Since 1983</Card>
```

Cards sit on tone (White on Paper, or the reverse) or a hairline border — not on
a shadow. `accent` adds the 3px gold top rule.
