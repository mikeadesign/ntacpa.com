What I collect

Two things, and nothing else. First, basic traffic measurement — which pages were viewed, when, roughly where the visit came from, and what kind of device it was. Second, whatever you choose to type into the inquiry form.

I do not sell information, share it with advertisers, or add anyone to a mailing list. There is no advertising on this site and no third-party tracking beyond the analytics described below.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages get read. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser — small files that let it recognise a returning visit. That data goes to Google and is subject to Google's own privacy terms.

You can refuse or delete these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on. Nothing on this site stops working if you do.

[CONFIRM] Whether IP anonymisation is switched on in your Analytics property, and whether you want a cookie consent banner. This site currently discloses cookies in the footer rather than asking for consent — appropriate for a US-only audience, but not sufficient if you expect visitors in the EU or UK.

The inquiry form

The form asks for a name, an email address, and a message. When you send it, that becomes an email to me. It is not stored in a database on this website, and no one else receives a copy.

Please don't put Social Security numbers, account numbers, or tax documents in a first message. Ordinary email is not a secure channel; once you're a client, I'll tell you how to send documents properly.

How long I keep it

Inquiry emails stay in my mail account while a conversation is live and for a reasonable period afterwards. Analytics data is retained on the schedule set in the Analytics property.

[CONFIRM] A stated retention period — for example, inquiry emails deleted after 12 months if no engagement follows, and Analytics set to 14 months. Naming a period is better than "a reasonable period."

Client records are different

This page is about the website. Tax documents, books, and anything else you give me as a client are handled under your engagement letter and the confidentiality rules that apply to a CPA — not under this notice. Those obligations are stricter, and they continue after an engagement ends.

Your requests

Write to contact@ntacpa.com to ask what I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. I'll answer myself.

[CONFIRM] Whether any state privacy statute applies to the practice — this depends on client residency and revenue thresholds, and it's the clause most worth an attorney's five minutes.
