Tax advice notice

What this website is, what it isn't, and where the line sits between reading a page here and being a client.

Nothing here is advice for your situation

The pages on this site describe the work I do in general terms. General is the operative word: a sentence that is true for most filers may be exactly wrong for you, and the useful answer almost always depends on facts that aren't on a website.

Do not act, or decline to act, on the basis of anything you read here. Ask me, or ask another qualified professional, about your own circumstances first.

You become a client when we sign, not before

Sending an inquiry does not create a professional relationship. Neither does a phone call, an email exchange, or my answering a question at no charge. An engagement begins when there is a written engagement letter naming the work, the period, and the fee, signed by both of us.

Until then I have no duty to act on a deadline you are facing, and you should not assume a filing is being handled.

Don't send sensitive information

The inquiry form and ordinary email are not secure channels. Please keep Social Security and taxpayer ID numbers, bank and brokerage account numbers, and copies of returns out of a first message. A description is enough — "three years unfiled, sole proprietor, Illinois" tells me what I need.

Once we're working together, I'll tell you how to send documents properly.

Links to other sites

Where this site links to the IRS, FINRA, a state revenue department, or any other outside source, it is for convenience. I don't control those pages, I'm not responsible for what they say, and a link is not an endorsement.

Past results are not a promise

Descriptions of past engagements — an examination resolved, a filing brought current, a net capital issue cleared — describe what happened in those matters. Facts differ, examiners differ, and the law changes. Nothing here should be read as a prediction about your outcome.

Written advice and Circular 230

Practice before the IRS is governed by Treasury Department Circular 230. Any written advice I give a client is prepared for that client's specific facts, and it isn't intended for anyone else to rely on or to use to avoid penalties.
