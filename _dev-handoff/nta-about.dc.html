Nicholas T. Avello, CPA

Four decades at the intersection of accounting and the markets.

I have been a Certified Public Accountant since 1983. Most of the forty-odd years since have been spent inside the securities and futures industries — as a registered Financial and Operations Principal, in the part of a brokerage where the numbers have to reconcile to the rule, not to an expectation.

NTA, Inc. has been my practice since 1990. It is deliberately one person. When you call, you get the accountant who prepared the return, and when a notice arrives three years later, you get the same one.

That is also why the client list looks unusual: hedge funds and broker-dealers on one side, individuals, families and small businesses on the other. The regulatory work keeps the standard high. The private work keeps it human.

Always, Never, Except For…

It has been my shorthand for this work for a long time. Every absolute in the tax code comes with a carve-out, and the carve-out is usually where your situation actually lives. Finding it is not a trick; it is reading, and then reading the part everyone skips.

Which is also why I will tell you when the answer is no. A firm that has filed accurately since 1983 stays in business by being right, not by being agreeable.
